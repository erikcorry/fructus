We will base malloc on cmpctmalloc, which is in $HOME/github/esp-idf/components/heap/third_party/dartino/cmpctmalloc.c

This has a license that lets us copy it into fructus (unlike the rest of esp-idf). It's by me.
We need to adapt it to a 16 bit environment.

It's a two level malloc. We will define pages to be 256 bytes. Any allocation > about 3/4 of a page
will come from the page allocator, and cmpctmalloc will also get its pages from there. Within pages
we use cmpctmalloc's bucket system. Allocations are two-byte rounded and aligned and within a page
all offsets are <256.  This means the allocations have a two byte header, one pointing to the next
allocation and one pointing at the previous one.  Free entries have the same header and a link
field for the freelist that is 16 bit so it can go from page to page.  The low bit of each header
byte is used for a free indicator.

Please take the code, remove esp-idf-isms and adapt it for fructus' small word size.  Assume no
locking is required. Unlike esp we don't allow allocation from interrupts and we won't have
premption within malloc.  We won't be attempting to upstream any cmpctmalloc changes so just slash
and burn. No ifdefs, this version of cmpctmalloc is for 16 bit fructus only. We'll only have
one cmpctmalloc heap and we can statically allocate it, loading its address in the prologue of
every malloc entry point and passing it to helper functions.
